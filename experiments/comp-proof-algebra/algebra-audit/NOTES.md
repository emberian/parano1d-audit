## Part 1: Galois Equivariance, Subfield Lattice & Sponge/IV Caller Audit

### 1.1 Binary Tower vs. Flat (GCM) Isomorphism & Parameter Subfield (`A1`)

- **Tower Definition (`noid_core/src/tower/block{8,16,32,64,128,256}.rs`)**:
  - `Block8` ($d=8$): $\mathrm{GF}(2)[z]/(z^8 + z^4 + z^3 + z + 1)$ (`0x11B`, AES polynomial).
  - Quadratic Artin-Schreier tower steps $X_m^2 + X_m + \tau_{m-1} = 0$ for $m \in \{4, 5, 6, 7, 8\}$:
    $$\tau_3 = \text{0x20},\quad \tau_4 = \text{0x2000},\quad \tau_5 = \text{0x2000\_0000},\quad \tau_6 = \text{0x2000\_0000\_0000\_0000},\quad \tau_7 = 2^{125}.$$
- **Flat Isomorphism (`noid_core/src/hardware.rs:256-340`)**:
  - `tower_to_flat_u128` ($T$) and `flat_to_tower_u128` ($T^{-1}$) apply $128\times 128$ $\mathrm{GF}(2)$-invertible matrices establishing a ring isomorphism between `Block128` and $\mathrm{GF}(2)[x]/(x^{128} + x^7 + x^2 + x + 1)$ (`clmul_gcm`), fixing $0$ and $1$.
- **Exact Parameter Subfield (`noid_poseidon2b/src/native/permutation.rs:185-206`)**:
  - All $4 \times 66 = 264$ `ROUND_CONSTANTS` take values in $\{0, 1, \dots, 7\} \subset \mathrm{GF}(2^8)$.
  - All $16$ entries of `MDS_FULL` take values in $\{1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\} \subset \mathrm{GF}(2^8)$.
  - `MDS_PARTIAL[0][0] = 0x20` also lies in $\mathrm{GF}(2^8)$; **only** the three passive diagonal entries `MDS_PARTIAL[1][1] = 0x2000`, `MDS_PARTIAL[2][2] = 0x0200`, and `MDS_PARTIAL[3][3] = 0x0800` lie in $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16}) \setminus \mathrm{GF}(2^8)$.
  - Consequently, $K = \mathrm{GF}(2^{16})$ is the **exact minimal subfield generated by all Poseidon2b parameters**, and the S-box $x \mapsto x^7$ commutes with every field automorphism.
- **Galois Equivariance Theorem**:
  - Every automorphism $\sigma_k(x) = x^{2^{16k}}$ ($k \in \{0, \dots, 7\}$) of $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})/\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})) \cong \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$ fixes all Poseidon2b round constants and MDS entries, hence commutes componentwise with the permutation $P$:
    $$P(\sigma_k(s_0), \sigma_k(s_1), \sigma_k(s_2), \sigma_k(s_3)) = \big(\sigma_k(P(s)_0), \sigma_k(P(s)_1), \sigma_k(P(s)_2), \sigma_k(P(s)_3)\big).$$
  - Verified bit-for-bit across `Poseidon2bPermutation::permute_mut` (`permutation.rs:29`), `permute_flat_u128` (`permutation.rs:50`), `packed_poseidon2b_permute` (`batch.rs:626`), `packed_poseidon2b_permute_flat` (`batch.rs:705`), and the 4-group interleaved PMULL kernel `packed_poseidon2b_permute_flat_many` (`batch.rs:651`, `batch_aarch64.rs:110-165`).
  - **Closed-Form Involutive Automorphism ($k = 4$, fixed field $\mathrm{GF}(2^{64})$)**: Since $X_6^2 + X_6 + \tau_5 = 0$ with $\tau_5 \in \mathrm{GF}(2^{64})$ and $\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathrm{GF}(2^{64})/\mathrm{GF}(2)}(\tau_5) = 1$, Frobenius $x \mapsto x^{2^{64}}$ maps $X_6 \mapsto X_6 + 1$. Thus for $v = \text{lo} + \text{hi}\cdot X_6$ in tower representation:
    $$\sigma_4(\texttt{Block128}(v)) = \texttt{Block128}(v \oplus (v \gg 64)).$$
- **Subfield Lattice Closure**:
  - Fixed fields of subgroups of $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})/\mathrm{GF}(2^{16}))$:
    - $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})^4$ ($64$-bit state, $32$-bit rate/digest): **invariant**;
    - $\mathrm{GF}(2^{32})^4$ ($128$-bit state, $64$-bit rate/digest): **invariant**;
    - $\mathrm{GF}(2^{64})^4$ ($256$-bit state, $128$-bit rate/digest): **invariant**;
    - $\mathrm{GF}(2)^4, \mathrm{GF}(2^2)^4, \mathrm{GF}(2^4)^4, \mathrm{GF}(2^8)^4$: **not invariant** (broken by `MDS_PARTIAL` passive diagonal).

### 1.2 Portability Defect in `Flat<Block128>` (`A4`, `noid_core/src/hardware.rs:647-767`)

- `Flat<Block128>::mul` (`hardware.rs:658-661`) unconditionally calls `clmul_gcm(self.0.0, rhs.0.0)` (flat polynomial basis).
- However, `Flat<Block128>::to_flat` (`hardware.rs:747-750`) and `to_tower` (`hardware.rs:762-765`) return `*self` (no-op) under `#[cfg(not(any(target_arch = "x86_64", target_arch = "aarch64")))]`, and `Flat<Block128>::invert` (`hardware.rs:726-729`) calls tower `Block128::invert()` directly without basis conversion!
- Moreover, `Flat<Block128>::EXTENSION_TAU` (`hardware.rs:715`) and `From<u32>`, `From<u64>`, `From<u128>` (`hardware.rs:695-709`) wrap raw tower bit-patterns without `tower_to_flat_u128` (only `0` and `1` coincide between tower and flat; even `2` differs).
- **Impact & Remediation**: Production targets (`x86_64` and `aarch64`) use `tower_to_flat_u128` / `F128` directly, so consensus/proving on supported CPUs is unaffected. Remove the `cfg` gate on `conversion_matrix` in `hardware.rs:718-766` (since `apply_matrix` is portable scalar Rust) and convert constants via `tower_to_flat_u128`.

### 1.3 Zero-IV Sponge Subspace Trap & `CryptographicHasher` IV-Drop (`A2`)

1. **Zero-IV Multi-Block Capacity Trap (Survives `0x80..01` Finalization Padding)**:
   - `Poseidon2bSponge::new()` (`compression.rs:58-65`), `poseidon2b_digest` (`digest.rs:10`), `CryptographicHasher::hash_concatenation` (`hasher_impl.rs:31`), and `batch::hash_concatenation_batch*` (`batch.rs:155, 239, 416`) initialize `(state[2], state[3]) = (0, 0) \in \mathrm{GF}(2^{16})^2$.
   - For any multi-block message whose first 32-byte rate block $A = (a_0, a_1)$ lies in $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})^2$ (or $\mathrm{GF}(2^d)^2$ for $d \in \{16, 32, 64\}$), the state after Block 0's permutation is $P(a_0, a_1, 0, 0) = (r_0, r_1, c_0, c_1) \in \mathrm{GF}(2^d)^4$.
   - Thus the 256-bit capacity $(c_0, c_1)$ is trapped in a **$2d$-bit subspace** ($32$ bits for $d = 16$), reducing inner-capacity collision resistance from the nominal $2^{128}$ birthday bound to **$2^d$** ($2^{16}$ for $d = 16$). Any capacity match before Block 1 can be cancelled in the rate lanes by Block 1's absorption prior to the final `0x80..01` padding block (`PAD1 = 1u128 << 120`).
2. **`CryptographicHasher` Silently Ignores `self`'s IV (`noid_poseidon2b/src/hasher_impl.rs:11-40`)**:
   - `hash_pair(&self, a, b)` computes `permute([*a, *b, 0, 0])[0..2]` (zero IV, no feed-forward, strictly $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})/\mathrm{GF}(2^{16}))$-equivariant, mapping $\mathrm{GF}(2^d)^2 \to \mathrm{GF}(2^d)^2$).
   - `hash_field(&self, elem)` and `hash_concatenation(&self, a, b)` instantiate fresh `Poseidon2bSponge::new()` (zero IV), completely ignoring `self.state` even when the caller constructed `Poseidon2bSponge::with_iv(capacity_iv(tag))`.
3. **Production Zero-IV Caller Inventory**:
   - **`noid_node/src/wallet/keystore.rs:79-89` (`MasterSecret::derive_spend_secret`)**: Calls `Poseidon2bSponge::with_iv([Block128::ZERO; 2])` on `(master_0, master_1)` followed by `(index, 0x6E6F69642D64657269)` and `finalize()`. Verified byte-for-byte identical to `poseidon2b_digest` and `CryptographicHasher::hash_concatenation` on the same 64 bytes (cross-primitive domain sharing). Safe for uniform 256-bit `master_secret` (probability $2^{-224}$ of `master_secret` falling in $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})^2$), but violates `CRYPTO.md §3`; replace `[Block128::ZERO; 2]` with `capacity_iv(DomainTag::new(b"KEYDERIV"))`.
   - **`noid_chain/src/fri_state.rs:251` (`compute_segment_root`, called via `SegmentedFriState` and `mdbx_context.rs:460`)**: Passes `Poseidon2bSponge::new()` into `interleaved_commit_cap` (`noid_fri_binius/src/interleaved_commit.rs:596, 654, 709-720`), which hashes segment leaves via zero-IV `hash_pair` / `hash_field`. *Reachability boundary*: consensus block headers and snapshot verification check the `exact` sparse-Merkle state root (`TAG_EXSTSLT` + 2-perm `TAG_EXSTNOD` at `exact_state_hash.rs:24-34`, `mdbx_context.rs:484`), whereas `seg_roots` is node-internal segment metadata.
   - **`noid_ivc_core/src/deep_chain/source_tree.rs:85`**: Uses zero-IV `hash_pair` for deep-chain test/helper source trees; production IVC commitments use `noid_ivc_core/src/merkle.rs` (`IVCPCSL_`, `IVCPCSF_`, `IVCPCSN_`).
   - **Remediation**: Deprecate zero-IV `Poseidon2bSponge::new()` and `poseidon2b_digest` in favor of `TAG_BYTEHASH` (`poseidon2b_hash_bytes`), give `hash_pair` / `hash_field` / `hash_concatenation` dedicated `DomainTag` capacity IVs (with feed-forward on 1-perm `hash_pair`), and give `MasterSecret::derive_spend_secret` its own `DomainTag` (with wallet migration version bump).

### 1.5 Quadratic Extension $\mathrm{GF}(2^{256})$ Challenge Map & $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathrm{GF}(2^{256})/\mathrm{GF}(2^{16}))$

## Part 2: Fiat-Shamir Channels, Bridges, Unframed Callers & `noid_soundness`

### 2.1 Channel State-Machine Audit (`T2`)

1. **Zero-Absorb Immediate Squeeze Ignores Capacity IV (`compression.rs:148-152, 190-196`, `channel.rs:79-88, 145-163`, `noid_fri/src/channel.rs:129-140`)**:
   - On a fresh `Poseidon2bChannel::new()`, `Poseidon2bWideChannel::new()`, or `noid_fri::channel::Channel::new()`, `sponge.filled_bytes == 0`. Calling `squeeze()` invokes `flush_to_squeeze()`, which is a **no-op** when `filled_bytes == 0`, and then `sponge.squeeze()` reads `[state[0], state[1]] = [0, 0]` **before** executing `permute()`!
   - Thus the first two base squeezes on any unabsorbed channel are identically `[0, 0]`, and `Poseidon2bWideChannel::new().squeeze_wide()` outputs constant `(0, Block128::TAU)` — completely independent of `TAG_KSCHANNL`, `TAG_KSCH256`, or `TAG_FRICHANL`.
   - Contrast: `FsChallenger` (`challenger.rs:429-435`) and `FsLaneChallenger` (`challenger.rs:671-684, 746, 868`) absorb `FS_OP_DOMAIN` at construction and `FS_OP_SQUEEZE` on every sample call, preventing 0-absorb squeezes.
2. **Odd-vs-Even Squeeze State Aliasing**:
   - In unframed `Poseidon2bChannel`, `noid_fri::channel::Channel`, and `Poseidon2bWideChannel::squeeze_base`, squeezing $2k - 1$ challenges vs $2k$ challenges executes the exact same number of permutations ($k$); a subsequent `absorb` (or `squeeze_wide`) clears `pending = None` (`pending_base = None`), yielding **identical post-operation states** despite distinct transcript interaction histories.
3. **Half-Block Misalignment of `Block256` in `zk_authorization.rs:974-1019`**:
   - Despite `domain.rs:86-89` stating *"Every algebraic message occupies one complete two-lane rate block"*, `init_main_channel`, `absorb_phase_b_prefix`, `absorb_mid_commitment`, and `absorb_tail` each absorb a **single 1-lane `Block128` tag** (`ZK_AUTH_MAIN_FROM_OWNER_TAG`, etc.) immediately before `absorb_wide(...)`, splitting every `Block256` across two rate blocks (`[tag, w0.lo]`, `[w0.hi, w1.lo]`, ..., `[wk.hi, PAD]`) and paying an extra pad-flush permutation per phase.
   - Prover, native verifier, and compiled recursive layout match in lockstep, and `close_into_bridge` (`zk_authorization.rs:560, 666`) always runs immediately after `squeeze_wide()` where `absorb_is_aligned() == true`, so soundness is preserved.
4. **`FsLaneChallenger` PoW Grinding Lockstep (`challenger.rs:779-804, 896-941`)**:

### 2.3 `noid_soundness` Accounting Audit (`S1`)

1. **Inverted `.max()` vs `.min()` Directionality in `noid_soundness/src/qrom.rs:43-55` (`history_query_squeeze_permutations`)**:
   - `qrom.rs:43-55` computes `s = history_query_squeeze_permutations(parameters)` using **`.max()`** across `parameters.history_classes` and passes `s` into `resource::certificate` (`resource.rs:295-318`), where `history.query` charges `scalar.sequential_permutations(s)`:
     $$\text{gate\_depth\_product}(s) = (G_1 \cdot s)(D_1 \cdot s) = s^2 \cdot G_1 D_1 \implies \text{bad\_density\_per\_gate\_depth} = \frac{\text{query\_escape}}{s^2 \cdot G_1 D_1}.$$
   - Because dividing by a **larger** $s^2$ makes `bad_density_per_gate_depth` **smaller** (more optimistic), conservative soundness across a multi-class HistoryStep bank requires **`.min()`** (charging the cheapest actual query-squeeze response), NOT `.max()`! Indeed, `noid_soundness/src/v2.rs:122-128` explicitly documents this exact rule (*"Use the cheapest actual column response, not the longest codeword's larger squeeze cost"*) and uses `.min()`.
   - **Quantitative Impact**: On legacy `[B25 (k_code=19), B255 (k_code=21)]` and 2-class v2 `[m=23 (k_code=20), m=24 (k_code=21)]`, $\lfloor 128 / k_{\text{code}} \rfloor = 6$ for both classes, giving $\lceil 133/6 \rceil = 23$ lanes and $\lceil 23/2 \rceil = 12$ permutations on both arms, accidentally masking the `.max()` bug. However, at **`m = 25` (`k_code = 22`)** — the v2.1 state-tree slot-expansion codeword size — $\lfloor 128 / 22 \rfloor = 5 \implies \lceil 133/5 \rceil = 27$ lanes $\implies \lceil 27/2 \rceil = \mathbf{14}$ permutations! On a mixed `[k_code=21 (12 perms), k_code=22 (14 perms)]` profile, `qrom.rs:51` charges $14^2 = 196$ instead of $12^2 = 144$, under-accounting `history.query` `bad_density_per_gate_depth` by exact factor **`196 / 144 = 49 / 36 = 1.361111x`** (and `history.query` is the active `limiting_event` in `lib.rs:86`!).
   - **Remediation**: Change `.max()` to `.min()` at `noid_soundness/src/qrom.rs:52`, and extend `noid_soundness::v2` to cover 3-class `v2::capacity_banked` (`[Small, Large, Native]`).
2. **Subfield-Blind Degree & Algebraic Work Bounds in `noid_soundness/src/poseidon2b_cryptanalysis.rs:203-253, 515-520, 622`**:
   - `poseidon2b_cryptanalysis::audit` sets `appendix_a_compression_applies = true` for `compress_flat_feed_forward_with_tag` and reports `ideal_degree_upper_bound = 7^73` ($\approx 2^{204.94}$), `quadratic_work_projection = 7^146` ($\approx 2^{409.88}$), and `nonlinear_subspaces` minimum algebraic work $\approx 2^{1022.83}$ using `capped_power` degree cap $2^{128}-2$ (`line 518`).
   - Simultaneously, `bivariate_resultant_screening` (`line 622`) computes `even_balancing_core = 0xbe32`, which visibly lies inside $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})$ (`< 65536`) precisely because all round constants and MDS entries lie in $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})$!
   - Over the invariant cosets $a \in T(\mathrm{GF}(2^d))^2$, $b \in T(\mathrm{GF}(2^d))^2 \oplus \text{iv\_flat}$ ($d \in \{16, 32, 64\}$), every univariate polynomial over $\mathrm{GF}(2^d)$ reduces modulo $X^{2^d} - X$ to degree $\le 2^d - 1$ ($65,535$ for $d = 16$, vs $7^{73}$), with birthday collision complexity $2^d$ ($2^{16}, 2^{32}, 2^{64}$) and coset preimage complexity $2^{2d}$ ($2^{32}, 2^{64}, 2^{128}$) on $\mathrm{GF}(2^d)^2$ digests.
   - **Remediation**: Either break subfield closure in `Poseidon2bPermutation` by drawing full-128-bit round constants that generate $\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})$ (not confined to $\mathrm{GF}(2^8)$ / $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})$), or explicitly restrict `poseidon2b_cryptanalysis.rs` claims to random/high-entropy input distributions (such as Merkle inner nodes whose children are uniform 256-bit sponge digests) and document the $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16}) / \mathrm{GF}(2^{32}) / \mathrm{GF}(2^{64})$ coset boundaries.
