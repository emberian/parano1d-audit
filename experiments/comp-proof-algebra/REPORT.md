### 2.1 Poseidon2b Parameter Subfield, $\mathrm{Gal}(\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})/\mathrm{GF}(2^{16}))$ Equivariance & Subfield Lattice

  - All $16$ entries of `MDS_FULL` lie in $\{1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7\} \subset \mathrm{GF}(2^8)$, and `MDS_PARTIAL[0][0] = 0x20` lies in $\mathrm{GF}(2^8)$.

  - Hence $K = \mathrm{GF}(2^{16})$ is the **exact minimal parameter subfield** of Poseidon2b, and the monomial S-box $x \mapsto x^7$ commutes with every field automorphism.

### 2.3 Detailed Cryptographic, Portability & Soundness-Model Findings (`PA-01`, `PA-05`, `PA-06`, `PA-07`, `PA-08`)

#### Finding `PA-01`: Inverted `.max()` vs `.min()` in `noid_soundness/src/qrom.rs:43-55` & Subfield-Blind Degree Cap in `poseidon2b_cryptanalysis.rs`
- **Citations:** `noid_soundness/src/qrom.rs:43-55`, `noid_soundness/src/resource.rs:295-318`, `noid_soundness/src/v2.rs:122-128`, `noid_soundness/src/poseidon2b_cryptanalysis.rs:203-253, 515-520, 622`.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. `qrom::history_query_squeeze_permutations` aggregates per-class query-seed squeeze permutations across `parameters.history_classes` using `.max()` (`qrom.rs:52`) and passes `s` into `resource::certificate` (`resource.rs:295-318`), which computes `gate_depth_product(s) = s^2 * G_1 * D_1` and **divides** `query_escape` by `gate_depth_product(s)` to obtain `bad_density_per_gate_depth`. Larger $s$ yields a *smaller* (more optimistic) density, so conservative multi-class accounting requires `.min()`, as `noid_soundness/src/v2.rs:122-128` explicitly documents (*"Use the cheapest actual column response, not the longest codeword's larger squeeze cost"*). On `[k_code=19, 21]` and `[k_code=20, 21]`, $\lfloor 128/k \rfloor = 6 \implies s = 12$ on both arms, masking the bug; at `m = 25` (`k_code = 22`, v2.1 slot-expansion size), $\lfloor 128/22 \rfloor = 5 \implies s = 14$, causing `.max()` to under-account `history.query` `bad_density_per_gate_depth` by **$14^2 / 12^2 = 49/36 \approx 1.3611\times$** (where `history.query` is the active `limiting_event` in `noid_soundness/src/lib.rs:86`).
  2. `poseidon2b_cryptanalysis.rs:203-253, 515-520` caps univariate degree at $2^{128}-2$ (`7^73` ideal degree) without accounting for the invariant $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})^4$ cosets (univariate degree $\le 2^{16}-1 = 65,535$), even though `nonlinear_subspaces.even_balancing_core` (`line 622`) evaluates to `0xbe32` $\in \mathrm{GF}(2^{16})$.
- **Remediation:** Change `.max()` to `.min()` at `noid_soundness/src/qrom.rs:52`, extend `noid_soundness::v2` to 3-class `capacity_banked`, and document coset-subfield boundaries in `poseidon2b_cryptanalysis.rs` (or use full-128-bit round constants generating $\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})$ in future protocol versions).

#### Finding `PA-05`: `Flat<Block128>` Basis-Conversion & `PackedBlock128` Portability Defect
- **Citations:** `noid_core/src/hardware.rs:647-767`, `noid_core/src/packed/arith.rs:37-88`.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. `Flat<Block128>::mul` (`hardware.rs:647-656`) unconditionally multiplies in flat GCM basis (`clmul_gcm`), whereas `Flat<Block128>::to_flat` (`lines 747-750`) and `to_tower` (`lines 762-765`) return `*self` (no-op) and `invert` (`lines 726-729`) calls tower `Block128::invert()` under `#[cfg(not(any(target_arch = "x86_64", target_arch = "aarch64")))]`. Because `PackedBlock128::packed_mul` (`packed/arith.rs:44-51`) and `scalar_mul` (`lines 76-86`) delegate to `Flat(a_tower).to_flat()`, `*`, and `.to_tower()`, packed tower multiplications produce incorrect field products on non-`x86_64`/`aarch64` targets (e.g., `wasm32`, `riscv64`), even though `tower_to_flat_u128` / `flat_to_tower_u128` (`packed/arith.rs:101-114`) are already portable scalar Rust.
  2. On **all** architectures, `Flat<Block128>::EXTENSION_TAU` (`hardware.rs:715`) and `From<u8/u32/u64/u128>` (`lines 690-709`) wrap raw tower bit-patterns without calling `tower_to_flat_u128` (only `0` and `1` coincide between tower and flat bases).
- **Remediation:** Remove the `#[cfg(...)]` target-arch gates in `Flat<Block128>::{invert, to_flat, to_tower}` (`apply_matrix` is already target-independent) and apply `tower_to_flat_u128` in `From<u8/u32/u64/u128>` and `EXTENSION_TAU`.

#### Finding `PA-06`: Zero-IV Multi-Block Sponge Subspace Property & `CryptographicHasher` IV-Drop
- **Citations:** `noid_poseidon2b/src/native/compression.rs:58-65`, `digest.rs:10-28`, `hasher_impl.rs:11-40`, `noid_node/src/wallet/keystore.rs:79-89`, `noid_chain/src/fri_state.rs:251`.
- **Mechanics:**
  - On `Poseidon2bSponge::new()` (capacity `[0, 0]`), any Block-0 rate pair $(a_0, a_1) \in \mathrm{GF}(2^d)^2$ ($d \in \{16, 32, 64\}$) maps $[a_0, a_1, 0, 0]$ into $\mathrm{GF}(2^d)^4$, confining the 256-bit post-Block-0 capacity $(c_0, c_1)$ to the $2d$-bit subspace $\mathrm{GF}(2^d)^2$ ($32$ bits for $d=16$), surviving final `0x80..01` padding for 2-block inputs.
  - `impl CryptographicHasher for Poseidon2bSponge` (`hasher_impl.rs:11-40`) ignores `self` on `hash_pair` (unwhitened 1-perm `permute([a, b, 0, 0])[0..2]`), `hash_field`, and `hash_concatenation`, discarding any `Poseidon2bSponge::with_iv(...)` state.
  - Production callers: `MasterSecret::derive_spend_secret` (`keystore.rs:79`) explicitly passes `[Block128::ZERO; 2]`, producing outputs byte-for-byte identical to `poseidon2b_digest` and `hash_concatenation` on the same 64 bytes (safe for uniform 256-bit master secrets, $2^{-224}$ probability of $\mathrm{GF}(2^{16})^2$ residency, but violates domain separation); `fri_state::compute_segment_root` (`fri_state.rs:251`, called during cold `mdbx_context::load_dense_chain_state` at `:460`) computes legacy `SegmentedFriState` `seg_roots`, whereas consensus headers authenticate `exact_state_hash` (`TAG_EXSTSLT` / `TAG_EXSTNOD`).
- **Remediation:** Deprecate zero-IV `Poseidon2bSponge::new()` / `poseidon2b_digest` in favor of `TAG_BYTEHASH`, bind `CryptographicHasher` methods to dedicated `DomainTag` IVs (with feed-forward on `hash_pair`), and assign a dedicated `DomainTag` to wallet key derivation on next keystore version bump.

## 3. Part II — In-Circuit Recursive Verifier (`self_verify.rs`), Sidecars & IVC Fold Audit

### 3.1 Detailed Circuit & PCS Findings (`PA-02`, `PA-03`, `PA-04`)

#### Finding `PA-02`: Missing `ff_merkle_booleanity_terms` in Walk-B `MerkleRegionVk` Zero Terms (`FeedForward` / `FeedForwardStrided`)
- **Citations:** `noid_ivc_core/src/deep_chain/ff_merkle.rs:224-239`, `noid_recursive/src/acceptance/trace/region_source_binding.rs:5481-5504`, `region_source_binding_c1.rs:77-118`, `region_source_binding_c1/trace_impl.rs:825-873`, `region_sidecar/merkle_trace.rs:177-216`, `region_sidecar.rs:799-831, 2446`.
- **Mechanics:**
  - `TwoPermutation` (`c1_merkle_zero_terms` lines `80-93`) and `PairedUpdate` (`lines 120-146`) enforce direction-bit booleanity (`EVEN * (D^2 + D) = 0`) inside Walk-B's own zero-check sumcheck.
  - By contrast, `FeedForward` and `FeedForwardStrided` emit only `ff_merkle_chain_terms`, leaving `pub fn ff_merkle_booleanity_terms` (`ff_merkle.rs:224-239`) with zero non-test callers across the workspace.
  - At each `ff_merkle` node, the permutation inputs are $L = (1+D)\,\mathrm{CR} + D\,\mathrm{SIB}$ and $R \oplus \mathrm{IV} = D\,\mathrm{CR} + (1+D)\,\mathrm{SIB}$. Over $\mathrm{GF}(2^{128})$, $\det \begin{pmatrix}1+D & D\\ D & 1+D\end{pmatrix} = (1+D)^2 + D^2 = 1$ for **every** $D \in \mathrm{GF}(2^{128})$. Consequently, for any honest pair $(L^*, R^*)$ and any $D \in \mathrm{GF}(2^{128}) \setminus \{0, 1\}$, setting $\mathrm{CR}' = (1+D)L^* + D R^*$ and $\mathrm{SIB}' = D L^* + (1+D) R^*$ leaves $(L, R)$ and all Walk-B `zero`, `substitution`, and `shift` sumcheck polynomials identically zero while binding an arbitrary point on a $2^{128}$-element affine line to the honest Merkle root.
  - **Production Mitigation vs. Standalone API Hazard:** All three production `HistoryStep` Walk-B instances (`Link` Walk L-B at `r_pcs_region.rs:1194-1196, 1644`; `Block` `wallet_b` and `meta_b` — including the 65th overflow query — at `region_source_binding.rs:1649, 1676`) allocate column 8 via outer R1CS boolean column allocators, completely closing this in consensus. However, `pub struct MerkleRegionVk` (`region_sidecar.rs:799`) does not enforce or document this external invariant, and its own standalone test (`region_sidecar.rs:2446`) allocates slice 8 via unconstrained `alloc_f128` (`circuit-audit` Check 2).
- **Remediation:** Include `ff_merkle_booleanity_terms(refs)` (with an independent $\lambda^3$ weight) inside `merkle_protocol_zero_terms{,_trace}` and `c1_merkle_zero_terms{,_trace}`, and document slice-8 booleanity on `MerkleRegionVk` until frozen VK pins are rotated.

#### Finding `PA-03`: Unconstrained Upper 64 Bits (`hi`) of `pow_nonce` in `self_verify.rs` & `verify_pow` Gadgets
- **Citations:** `noid_recursive/src/acceptance/trace/self_verify.rs:1595, 1780`, `noid_ivc_core/src/field_circuit.rs:1847-1860, 2561-2580`, `noid_recursive/src/acceptance/history_step/gated_recorder.rs:325-341`, contrasted with `noid_recursive/src/acceptance/trace/zk_auth_nonce.rs:4-14, 40-63`.
- **Mechanics:**
  - Native `BaseFoldProof::pow_nonce` / `C1BaseFoldProof::pow_nonce` is `u64`, absorbed as `F128 { lo: nonce, hi: 0 }` (`challenger.rs:930-941`).
  - `self_verify.rs:1595, 1780` allocates `pow_nonce` via unconstrained 128-bit `b.alloc_f128(...)`, and when `bits > 0` (`QUERY_GRIND_BITS = 16`), all three circuit `verify_pow` implementations decompose only the squeezed challenge `pt` (`128 - bits = 112` bits) with zero constraints forcing `nonce.hi == 0` (`circuit-audit` Check 3).
  - By contrast, `zk_auth_nonce.rs:4-14, 40-63` explicitly enforces a 64-bit boolean decomposition on `zk_authorization`'s `u64` grind nonce so recursive witnesses cannot use upper bits unrepresentable in native serialized proofs.
  - **Impact:** Soundness-preserving for consensus PoW cost (every 128-bit lane still traverses the full Poseidon2b permutation and 16-bit leading-zero gate), but widens the recursive predecessor witness relation relative to the native serialized `C1FieldR1csProof` wire format.
- **Remediation:** Add `b.decompose_bits_le(nonce, 64);` inside `verify_pow_trace`, `FsChannelUnionRecorder::verify_pow`, and `BaseSelectableParentRecorder::verify_pow` when `bits > 0`.

#### Finding `PA-04`: Unabsorbed `post_row_batch_commit` (`T2` Root) in C0 BaseFold at `log_batch_size == 0`
- **Citations:** `noid_ivc_core/src/pcs/basefold.rs:972, 1101, 1271-1284`, `noid_recursive/src/acceptance/trace/self_verify.rs:2281-2297` (fixed in C1 at `basefold.rs:1915-1917` and `self_verify.rs:2527-2530`).
- **Mechanics:**
  - `verifier.rs:1093, 1192` and `self_verify.rs:3343` allow `log_batch_size == 0`. In C0 `basefold::verify` and `basefold_verify_trace`, `observe_root(challenger, &proof.post_row_batch_commit.root)` executes only inside `for round in 0..log_msg_len` when `round + 1 == log_batch_size && !arities.is_empty()`. At `log_batch_size == 0`, `round + 1 >= 1`, so `post_row_batch_commit` (`T2`) is **never absorbed into the Fiat-Shamir transcript** (`circuit-audit` Check 1), while `basefold::prove` underflows `0usize - 1` at `basefold.rs:972, 1101`.
  - Production `HistoryStep` (`log_batch_size = 5` on C1) and SPARK retirement (`sparse_c1.rs:116-120`, which hits `log_batch_size == 0` at `log_len == 3` on **C1**) are protected by C1's pre-loop guard `if log_batch_size == 0 && !arities.is_empty() { observe_root(...); }`.
- **Remediation:** Backport C1's pre-round-0 `observe_root` guard to C0 `basefold::verify`, `basefold_verify_trace`, and `basefold::prove` (or reject `log_batch_size == 0` in C0).
